What's New

Every update, straight from the source. Latest version: v6.0.0.

v6.0.0 Latest Queue overhaul, lyrics polish, playback clean-up · Oct 23, 2026 · 21 MB
Queue
  • Fresh sessions: playing a song from search now starts a clean single-track session — the whole results list no longer floods your queue.
  • Real playlist order: in stable (non-shuffle) mode, “Next up” plays your queue in the exact order and radio suggestions are hidden.
  • YouTube-style radio: shuffle keeps the “Up next” suggestions, with your own queue listed underneath.
Player
  • Shuffle & Repeat are back as separate buttons on the player.
  • Video toggle removed: YouTube stopped serving playable video URLs, so it only produced dead ends and failed playback.
Lyrics
  • Fixed skipped lines: synced lyrics no longer silently skip lines that use [mm:ss] timestamps without a fraction.
  • Centre-stage lyrics: the active line now stays centred on screen as the song plays, instead of sticking to the top edge.
Stability
  • Leaner playback engine: the stream-resolution stack is inlined into the app — fewer moving parts, smaller installs, properly signed release pipeline.
  • Forward-compatible: version 6.0.0 (8) can install cleanly over this in future updates, no reinstall needed.

v4.5.1 Home redesign & performance · May 31, 2026 · 20 MB · 1,655 downloads
Home Screen
  • Section-based feed: Keep Listening, Quick Picks, Forgotten Favorites, Recommendations, and YouTube.
  • Time-based greeting (Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Night) with the Raazi branding pill.
  • Keep Listening: full-width resume card for your most recent track plus compact horizontal recents.
  • Local interaction metrics rank your home feed smarter over time.
Search
  • Suggestion debounce cut from 300ms to 150ms.
  • Network calls moved to the IO dispatcher — no more main-thread blocking.
  • 50-entry LRU cache for instant repeat queries.
New Features
  • Spotify playlist import — one tap, no more losing your collections.
  • Ringtone trimmer — cut songs into ringtones, alarms, and notifications.
  • Gemini resolver helper.
Stability
  • Stream-resolution timeout protection.
  • Lifecycle guards against background crashes.
  • Crash-safe database initialization.
  • Thread-safe YouTube music extraction.
